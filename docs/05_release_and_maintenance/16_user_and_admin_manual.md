## 16.1 Administrator Portal Guide (`/admin`)

### 1. Council Member Management
1. Navigate to `/admin/councils`.
2. To add a new member, click **"Add Council Member"**.
3. Select Council Type:
   - **Permanent Council** (Founders, Chief Coordinators, Advisory)
   - **Executive Council** (President, General Secretary, Executive Members)
   - **Temporary Council** (Audit, Discipline, Election)
4. Upload photo (automatically compressed by Cloudinary) and enter contact details.
5. Click **"Save Member"**.

---

### 2. Exporting Member Roster to Excel
1. In `/admin/members`, select the desired branch (e.g. `GSTU` or `All Branches`).
2. Click the green **"Export to Excel (.xlsx)"** button.
3. The system instantly downloads the stylized spreadsheet to your device and automatically saves a backup copy to CRC's Google Drive.

---

### 3. Publishing Audit Reports & Financial Disclosures
1. Navigate to `/admin/transparency`.
2. Click **"Upload Audit Document"**.
3. Attach the Chartered Accountant Audit Report (PDF).
4. Enter the Fiscal Year (e.g. `2024-2025`) and Report Title.
5. Click **"Publish"** — the report becomes immediately downloadable by the public on the website and is mirrored to Google Drive.
