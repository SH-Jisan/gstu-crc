## 1.1 Context & Background
**Come for Road Child (CRC)** originated on **5 June 2016** as a youth-driven volunteer humanitarian movement at Gopalganj Science and Technology University (GSTU), founded by Md. Rasel Ahmed and Md. Sakib Ahmed. Operating under the slogan *"The World Will Be Free from Street Children by Connected Together"*, CRC has expanded into multiple university chapters (GSTU, Khulna Central, Islamic University, Barisal University, Mawlana Bhashani STU) and urban centers.

Its institutional educational program, **Hatekhori School** (founded 2017), alongside emergency relief, winter clothing distribution, and child health camps, directly impacts hundreds of disadvantaged children.

---

## 1.2 Core Problem Statement
Despite its significant grassroots impact, CRC faces three operational and technological bottlenecks:

1. **Information Asymmetry & Public Trust Deficit**:
   - Potential donors and institutional partners lack an official, centralized digital portal to inspect audited financial reports, constitutional bylaws, and governance structures.
   - Without high Google search visibility (`gstu-crc.org`) and verified digital badges, fundraising campaigns on social media suffer from skepticism.

2. **Decentralized & Manual Branch Operations**:
   - Volunteer applications, membership data, and activity logs across multiple university branches are handled via ad-hoc spreadsheets and chat groups.
   - There is no automated mechanism to compile real-time, consolidated member rosters into standardized Excel formats.

3. **Absence of a Safeguarded Child Reporting Channel**:
   - When vulnerable or abused street children are spotted, citizens and volunteers lack a direct, secure, and confidential digital channel ("Report a Concern") to notify the designated safeguarding committee without public exposure.

---

## 1.3 Solution Vision
Build an authoritative, high-speed, and secure web platform (**`https://gstu-crc.org`**) combining:
- High-converting, transparent donation workflows with automated voucher generation.
- Interactive branch maps and educational impact showcases (Hatekhori School).
- Whistleblower-safe child safeguarding reporting.
- Enterprise-grade administrative control with automated Excel reporting and mirrored Google Drive cloud vaults.
